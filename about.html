About Chiya Ghar

Chiya Ghar started as a single stove and four stools beside a stationery shop. We wanted a place where students, shopkeepers and travellers could share a good cup of tea without hurry.

Today we still buy leaves directly from growers, pay them fairly, and cook our snacks fresh each morning. We keep the menu short so that we can keep the quality high.

Our journey

  • 2019
    One stove, four stools, one pot of masala chiya.
  • 2021
    First direct order of green tea from Ilam.
  • 2023
    Moved into our current shop with a reading shelf.
  • 2025
    Started weekly tea-tasting evenings.

What we care about

Fair prices

Growers are paid before the season ends, not after.

Less waste

We serve in steel cups and compost every tea leaf.

Open to all

Everyone is welcome, whether you order one cup or five.
